Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the free trial?

Your free trial starts from the moment you activate your account, and is valid for 14 days. During the trial period, you will have up to 3 user licences.
You can connect Facebook Messenger, WeChat, LINE, emails, and install Web Widget with Live Chat and Web Call – all free of charge, with no usage fees. No charges will be incurred for staff-to-staff calls, chats, group chats, video calls, or audio/video conference calls made through CINNOX.
You will have the same high standards of security, SLA of 99.95%, access to our customer success team, and 24/7 technical support.

What is a Licence?

A CINNOX licence is assigned to one named staff member and provides access to the CINNOX platform based on your subscribed plan.

One staff member can sign in on multiple devices using the same licence, with one active call at a time.
‍
When upgrading from the free trial to a paid plan, any staff licences created during the trial will be carried over with their staff names and settings. These licences will convert into paid licences based on the minimum licence requirement of the selected plan.

Is there a minimum number of licences?

Yes. The minimum number of licences vary by plan:

Free Trial
• Up to 3 licences
‍• 14-day trial period
‍
Digital Commerce Plan
• Minimum 3 paid licences

Omnichannel Contact Center
• Minimum 5 paid licences

‍The Ultimate CX Hub
• Minimum 10 paid licences

Do you charge any usage or setup costs?

Usage costs will be incurred for the following:
- Number of SMS sent
- Outbound calls made to mobile numbers or landlines per minute
- Incoming calls to toll-free and local DID numbers per minute
- Participants dialling into conference calls through a toll-free or local DID number (if applicable)
- Audio Transcription (speech-to-text service) based on the amount of audio successfully processed each month.
- The charge for add-on items is based on monthly usage.
‍
View the detailed usage rates on our Pricing page.
‍
Setup, purchase, or recurrent costs will be incurred for the following items:
- Virtual number, WhatsApp number, Alphanumeric sender ID
- Conference invite via SMS or Dial-out
- IP/number allow list or block list
- IVR menu, SIP Trunk Connection, SIP In/Out
- Bring your own carrier
- Removal of CINNOX logo on widget footer
- Consultancy services

Which chatbot platforms do you support?

CINNOX supports the integration with various chatbot platforms, including Azure OpenAI and Google Dialogflow. Additionally, any chatbot platform can be integrated using our General Chatbot Integration Method. This allows you to custom-design chatbot interactions for lead qualification, intelligent response automation, and your virtual assistant.

How do you protect my customer data?

CINNOX has a comprehensive security and authentication policy that covers all aspects of the organization, internal and external communication, and operating systems. We employ state-of-the-art data encryption, data backup and recovery, and data protection systems that safeguard you and your customers at all times.

How can I pay?

Currently, we accept Mastercard and Visa. All prices are in USD.
