# Customer Type Clarifier

When an unknown customer contacts you:

1. Ask: "Are you an existing {{classification.brand_short_name}} customer, or is this your first time reaching out?"
2. Based on their answer:
   - New → proceed to discovery questions
   - Existing → ask for account identifier
   - Partner → route to partner support
