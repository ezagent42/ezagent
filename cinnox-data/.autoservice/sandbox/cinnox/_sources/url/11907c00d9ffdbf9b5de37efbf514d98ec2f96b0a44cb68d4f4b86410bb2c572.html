A smarter way to improve customer experiences

CINNOX gives your team ultra-flexible, rule-based routing on a single platform. With smart data capture and real-time analysis, we ensure every inquiry is handled efficiently, reducing wait times and improving customer support. Our digital platform supports a wide range of routing methods to fit your unique business needs.

• Location Routing: Connect customers to the nearest or most relevant team based on their location (IP address).

• Language Routing: Automatically route inquiries to agents who speak the visitor's preferred site or device language.

• Time-Based Routing: Ensure customers are served during appropriate business hours based on their time zone or your team's availability.

• Percentage-Based Routing: Distribute call volume across teams or agents according to pre-set percentages for optimal workload balance.

• Sticky Routing: Offer personalized continuity by assigning returning visitors to the same agent who handled their previous inquiry.

By integrating these smart routing features into your customer engagement platforms, you can deliver seamless, context-aware support that keeps customers satisfied and teams productive.
